3. Information We Process on Your Device

Battery Forensics queries standard Android system APIs to provide real-time battery and power diagnostics. All data collected is stored exclusively in your device's private application sandbox:

  • Battery State & Health Metrics: Charge percentage (%), temperature (°C / °F), voltage (mV), health status, charging technology (USB, AC, Wireless), and calculated battery degradation trends based on charging delta benchmarks.
  • Hardware Power Telemetry: CPU frequency states, screen-on time vs. screen-off standby power drain, detected kernel wakelocks, and peripheral counts (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth).
  • User-Generated Tests & Notes: User benchmark test duration, custom timeline annotations, and saved charger profiles.

4. Android Device Permissions

The app requests only permissions necessary for battery monitoring and user-initiated alerts:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used solely to deliver user-configured battery alerts (high temperature warning alerts, 80% charge completion notifications, and deep discharge warnings).
  • BATTERY_STATS / PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Optional / Developer Mode): Used to inspect per-app energy consumption when granted via ADB or system developer access.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE (Optional): Correlates cellular radio standby states with battery drain. Does not access call logs or contact records.
  • BLUETOOTH_SCAN & BLUETOOTH_CONNECT (Optional): Used to count active Bluetooth peripherals and calculate estimated peripheral power draw.
  • VIBRATE: Provides haptic feedback when hardware diagnostic tests start or complete.
